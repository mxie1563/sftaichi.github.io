Why Practice Tai Chi?

Discover the transformative power of "meditation in motion" — master your flow.

🧘

Improve Self Awareness

Develop heightened body awareness through slow, deliberate movements that build a harmonious connection to the Chi — all things alive in your mind and in your physical world.

⚖️

Enhanced Balance & Stability

Strengthen your core and leg muscles, learn basic self-defense movements, significantly reducing the risk of falls and improving overall body control and self-confidence.

⚡

Profound Stress Reduction

The meditative, continuous movements help you find your center of balance, listen, read, and cultivate the Chi — helping you manage anxiety and achieve mental clarity. Learn breathing techniques to boost your body's natural energy flow and improve vitality.

Meet Your Instructor & Community

Sifu Michael Xie

Master of the Internal Arts

Sifu Michael has been a devoted practitioner of Tai Chi Chuan for over 28 years. He specializes in the gentle, yet powerful, Hun Yuan Chen Style. His teaching philosophy focuses on making the complex art accessible to all ages, emphasizing proper structure and meditative focus over strenuous effort.

"The greatest journey is the one back to yourself."
